波巴 Egypt’s Boba Tea Pioneers

25+ years of retail craft — poured into every cup.

Born from a childhood obsession and shaped by over 25 years of world-class Apple retail hospitality, PÀO brought authentic Taiwanese boba culture to Egypt in 2023. Every cup is brewed fresh, every pearl simmered warm in small batches, and every visit designed as your everyday escape.

01 / FRESHNESS

Made fresh every hour.

Small-batch black tapioca simmered warm in muscovado, tea leaves steeped clean — never powdered shortcuts.

02 / AUTHENTICITY

Real ceremonial craft.

First-harvest Japanese matcha whisked to order, roasted taro root, and specialty espresso pulled fresh.

03 / JOY

Happiness in every sip.

From the snap of piercing our orange bear seal lid to sharing croffles and udon boxes with friends.

04 / ACCESS

Zero-sugar & 24/7 bars.

Customize sweetness down to 0% Skinny monkfruit & oat milk — or roll through our 24/7 Drive-Thrus anytime.
